The Dio (Digital Input/Output) driver module manages digital input and output operations of the microcontroller, serving as an interface to the physical digital pins.

The Dio driver module enables control of digital pins, allowing for read and write operations where elements can be easily accessed by user-defined symbolic names.

Pins are organized into ports, consisting of multiple channels. Channels represent individual pins, while channel groups enable simultaneous access to multiple channels. All channels must be neighbors to each other in the channel group.
