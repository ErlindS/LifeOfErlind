## **What are the Sources of Additional Information?**
In previous modules you have already gotten familiar with technical references that are provided within the Doc folder inside your SIP. Vector provides content created by experts in the AUTOSAR, Vector Tooling and production projects field. Technical references are one of the sources of additional information that you will need while working on your project.  
This lesson provides you with an overview of the location of the information that you will need as well as the context where it will be applied. It will also guide you through the access infrastructure to the existing content step-by-step.
Now, let’s explore the ever-growing knowledge world that Vector provides to its customers.

## **What is Vector Customer Portal?**
The customer portal offers access to the Vector support platform, which you can use to submit issues with Vector products to our support team and to gain access to confidential content for Vector customers. The system also offers an overview of your existing support tickets and their status. Help will be provided by your local Vector office.

## **What is a Vector E-Training?**
Our e-learning platform is free of charge and a user account and/or login are not required. Here you can find an introduction to various bus technologies, e.g. CAN, Ethernet, FlexRay, LIN, J1939 as well as AUTOSAR topics. 
The content of the e-learning platform is also suitable as preparation of some training courses.

## **What is the Vector Knowledge Base?**
While working with Vector tools and/or within the AUTOSAR context, various questions may frequently arise. Our service team collects and documents them in the Vector Knowledge Base.
The Vector Knowledge Base offers worldwide access and contains useful information about Vector tools. It also contains hints, known errors, documentation, procedures on how to install software and other useful information.

## **What are Vector Webinars?**
Some topics are easier to explain through spoken word, hence the use of videos or presentations. Because of that, we and our partners create webinars from time to time. You can join them live, but if they can't fit your schedule, you can watch them later, since we keep all of our webinars available online. 
In these webinars certain topics are presented in detail (e.g. new product features, configuration concepts, etc.) and you have the possibility to follow along from your work place.

## **Which documents are included in the MICROSAR SIP delivery?**
All delivery-related documents are contained in the doc folder of your MICROSAR SIP. The provided documents correspond to the scope of your delivery. This means that only those documents relevant to your project are available in your delivery.
The doc folder contains the following subfolders: 
## ApplicationNotes
Application Notes describe specific topics or use cases important for your software.
**Example**: AN-ISC-8-1149_ErrorHook_E_OS_DISABLED_INT.pdf 
## DeliveryInformation
The subfolder DeliveryInformation provides general information about your MICROSAR delivery, e.g.: 
````
- The **delivery description** contains information about the delivered MICROSAR modules, the release type of your MICROSAR delivery and contact information.  
    **Example**: DeliveryDescription_<CBD-number>.html
- The **issue report** contains all open issues for the delivered MICROSAR modules.  
    **Example**: IssueReport_<CBD-number>.pdf
- The **issue handling** of third party modules are described in the issue handling documents.  
    **Example**: IssueHandling_<hardware>_MCAL.pdf)
- The **product information** contains general product-, program- and microcontroller-specific information:
    - The **product-specific product information** contains general information about a MICROSAR delivery, warranty, issue reporting, information about licensing, release types and more. In addition, the product-specific product information defines important constraints and limitations of MICROSAR modules that may have been delivered.  
        **Example**: ProductInformation_2_MICROSAR4.pdf
    - The **program-specific product information** contains program-specific information about the supported specifications as well as important constraints and limitations of program-specific modules.  
        **Example**: ProductInformation_2_<OEM>_<SLP>.pdf
    - The **microcontroller-specific product information** contains important information, constraints and limitations of MICROSAR modules in the context of a specific microcontroller.  
        **Example**: ProductInformation_2_<microcontroller vendor>.pdf
`````

## SafetyManuals
Depending on the release type, the following documents are stored in the subfolder SafetyManuals:
- The **safety manual** describes the assumptions made by Vector during the development of MICROSAR Safe as Software Safety Element out of Context (SEooC). This document provides information on how to integrate MICROSAR Safe into a safety-related project. This document is intended for the user of MICROSAR Safe.  
    **Example**: Safety_Manual_CBD-number_D##.pdf
- The **safety case** confirms to the user of MICROSAR Safe that all activities required by ISO 26262 have been completed by Vector.  
    **Example**: SafetyCase_E2E.pdf

## TechnicalReferences
Technical references are documents from developers for developers and contain detailed technical information about a MICROSAR module or module-spanning topics, API tables, restrictions and limitations. They also contain the integration and configuration instructions of how other modules should be configured in order to run the module in a MICROSAR stack.  
**Example**: TechnicalReference_Com.pdf

## UserManuals
User Manuals are guiding documents, including e.g. basic introduction to MICROSAR modules, use case descriptions and cross-module topics.  
**Example**: UserManual_AMD.pdf  
A special form of a User Manual is the startup manual. A startup manual is OEM-specific and describes step by step, how to start with your MICROSAR project and how to get the delivery basically running. A startup manual also contains conceptual information so you get familiar with MICROSAR modules and tools like DaVinci Developer Classic and DaVinci Configurator.  
**Example**: Startup_<OEM>_<SLP>.pdf
