
**What are Vector Services?**
Besides products and tools portfolio, Vector offers various services that provide you with optimal assistance for your project. This services are listed below:
- Support Service @ Vector
- Coaching Service @ Vector
- Vector Academy 
This e-learning platform is part of them, i.e. Vector Academy.

What is Support Service @ Vector?
Support Service @ Vector (or Help Desk) represents a ticketing system where you can ask Vector your question via Vector Portal or e-mail. In later parts of this module, we will go through usage of the Vector Portal as well as how to contact the support optimally. 
Support Service is event-triggered and focuses on providing first aid, fast reaction times and troubleshooting.  

## What is Coaching Service @ Vector?
For some specific questions or requests for your project, the support (or Help Desk) can be insufficient. In this case Vector has a special coaching service that represents extended support and direct peer-to-peer contact to one of the Vector coaches. 
Coaching service usually focuses on project accompaniment, know-how transfer, conceptual work or taking a part in work packages of your project. 

## **Difference between Support and Coaching Service**
Sometimes it is quite hard to tell the difference between coaching and support service. Although the difference is quite blurred regarding the content, from the time consumption point of view it can get more noticeable.
Up next, an overview of the main differences between support and coaching Service @ Vector.

Support Service
- **E-mail Support:** Yes, unknown contact person
- **Main Focus:** Troubleshooting
- **Request Type:** Event-driven request which requires short response time
- **Project Details:** Out of scope and independent of other requests
- **Contact:** Impersonal, via e-mail or Vector Portal
- **On-site support:** Not available
- **Time Limit:** Few hours 
- **Examples:** Installation support, configuration support

Coaching Service
- **E-mail Support:** Yes, dedicated person - Vector coach
- **Main Focus:** Training on project, consulting, know-how transfer
- **Request Type:** Must be planned, mid and long-term activities (e.g. work packages)
- **Project Details:** Known
- **Contact:** Personal, via phone or e-mail
- **On-site support:** Included if necessary
- **Time Limit:** Based on budget
- **Examples:** Features beyond "off-the-shelf" product, performance optimization, application design
## **What is Vector Academy?**
Besides support and coaching, you can also get training for some specific topic that you want to gather more knowledge about. Vector offers numerous seminars and workshops dealing with Vector software tools, Vector standard software components, AUTOSAR as well as current bus technologies and protocols. The classes are typically organized in such a way that they balance theory with practice. The training organized through Vector Academy is held in Vector training facilities.
